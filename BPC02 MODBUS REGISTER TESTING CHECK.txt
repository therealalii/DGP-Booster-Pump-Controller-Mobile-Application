========================================

READ-ONLY TESTING ONLY.

Do not use:
FC05 — Write Single Coil
FC06 — Write Single Register
FC15 — Write Multiple Coils
FC16 — Write Multiple Registers

Do not change pump activation settings.
Do not change hardware configuration.
Do not activate manual outputs.

The manual describes writing 1234 to address 99
to enter manual mode. Do not perform this operation
during register discovery.
